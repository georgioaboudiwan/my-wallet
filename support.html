Support

Track your money, plan your month and split costs with friends and family, in any currency.

Quick help

  • Ask Gio: tap the Gio button at the top of the app and ask anything about your money or how the app works.
  • Tour and guide: Settings, Help and tour.
  • A balance looks wrong: open the account and tap Change balance, then say where the difference came from.
  • Forgot your password: use Forgot password on the sign-in screen.
  • New phone: sign in with the same email; your data syncs.

Contact

Found a bug or have an idea? Write on the BudgetSplit support page. Please don't post amounts, names or other personal details there.
