Delete your account

You can delete your BudgetSplit account and all of its data yourself, at any time, from the app or from any web browser. You don't need to install anything.

How to delete it

  1. Open BudgetSplit on your phone, or open the web app in any browser and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings, then Account.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Enter your password (not needed if you sign in with Google or Microsoft) and type DELETE to confirm.

What gets deleted

Everything, right away and permanently: your account and email, all accounts, entries, categories, budgets, savings goals, people, groups you created, shared links, receipt photos, reminders, notification addresses, Gio question counts and usage events.

If you were part of someone else's group or shared loan, their own copy of the shared history stays with them.

Copies inside our hosting provider's backups are overwritten within a few days. Nothing is kept after that.

Want a copy first?

Before deleting, you can download your entries or a full backup in Settings, Sync and backup.

Can't sign in?

Reset your password from the sign-in screen, then follow the steps above. If that doesn't work, ask for deletion on the BudgetSplit support page without posting personal details; we will reply with a private way to confirm the account is yours.
