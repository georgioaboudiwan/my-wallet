BudgetSplit

Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026

BudgetSplit is a personal budget and bill-splitting app made by Georgio Aboudiwan. This page explains what the app collects, why, where it goes and how you control it. There are no ads, and your data is never sold.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and a password (or your Google or Microsoft sign-in) so you can log in.
  • Your money data: accounts, entries, categories, budgets, plan, savings goals, the people and groups you split with, investments and settings.
  • Receipt photos you choose to attach.
  • Notification address of a device, only if you turn on reminders.
  • Usage events: which screens and features are used (for example "opened Insights" or "added an entry"), the device type (iPhone, Android, computer), whether the app is installed, and the app version. These never contain amounts, names or notes. They help fix problems and decide what to improve.

Encryption

Names, amounts, notes, balances, your plan and your settings are encrypted on your device with a key made from your password before they are saved. The server stores only unreadable text, and the developer cannot read it.

Some details are not encrypted so the app can work: dates, currencies, which entry belongs to which account or category (as codes, not names), receipt photos (private to your account), your email, sign-in dates and how many records you have.

Everything travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Gio, the AI assistant

Gio is optional. When you ask Gio a question or ask it to add an entry, your question and a short summary of your numbers (this month's totals, the names of your categories, accounts and people, account and people balances, upcoming bills and recent entries) are sent to Groq, an AI service, to write the answer. Nothing is sent until you ask. BudgetSplit does not store the conversation. Groq does not keep it by default, though it may keep it for up to 30 days when it investigates errors or abuse (see Groq's data policy). The server only counts how many questions you asked today, to apply the daily limit. Gio can make mistakes and is not financial advice.

Other services we use

  • Supabase hosts the database, sign-in, file storage and server functions.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the app files.
  • Groq answers Gio questions, as described above.
  • Gmail sends invitation emails and the optional monthly report. The monthly report contains last month's totals by category, which your device sends only when the report is on. You can turn it off in Settings.
  • Apple, Google and Mozilla push services deliver notifications if you turn them on.
  • Exchange rate and price services (open.er-api.com, CoinGecko, gold-api.com, Yahoo Finance) are asked for public rates and prices. Nothing about you is sent to them.

Sharing with people

If you share a loan or a group with someone, they see the shared balance and its history, and nothing else. A private page you create for a person can be opened by anyone with its link; you can turn the link off at any time.

How long we keep it

Your data is kept while your account exists. When you delete your account, your account, entries, receipts, shared links, reminders and usage events are deleted permanently. Backups held by our hosting provider are overwritten within a few days.

Your choices and rights

  • Export your entries or a full backup at any time in Settings, Sync and backup.
  • Turn off reminders, the monthly email and Gio at any time.
  • Delete your account and all its data in Settings, Account, Delete account. See how to delete your account.
  • Ask what data we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, using the contact below.

Children

BudgetSplit is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date, and important changes will be announced in the app.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open the app and go to Settings, Help, or write on the BudgetSplit support page.
